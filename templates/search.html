Search syntax & examples
Multiple words
Results must contain all the words you type.
samuel froehlich — episodes containing both terms.
Word variants (stemming)
Common endings are folded together automatically.
martyr matches martyr, martyrs, martyrdom.
baptism matches baptize, baptized, baptizing.
Partial words (prefix)
A short word matches anything starting with it.
mart matches martyr, martin, martha.
Note: matching happens on word stems, so a query like ante may also surface anti… words because they share a stem. Highlighted words in the snippet show why each result matched.
Common words ignored
Stop words like the, a, of, and, to are skipped. Search by content words instead.
Case
Search is case-insensitive. God and god match the same things.
Punctuation
Punctuation is treated as a word break. Hyphens and em-dashes split a phrase into separate words.
Linkable searches
You can deep-link a query with ?q=.
/pages/search?q=baptism
